Most traders focus on **strategy, entries, exits, and indicators**.  
But one silent factor quietly eats into profits every single day — **brokerage**.

At first glance, ₹20 here or 0.03% there doesn’t feel like much.  
But over time, brokerage **adds up**, especially for active traders.

---

## How Brokerage Adds Up Over Time

Imagine this:

- You place **10–20 trades per day**
- You trade **intraday, options, or scalping strategies**
- You frequently adjust positions, hedge, or roll contracts

Each action is charged **per order or per lot** by most brokers.

### The Result?
Even a profitable trader can end up paying **thousands to lakhs per year** in brokerage — money that could have stayed in your trading account.

---

## Why Brokerage Matters More Than You Think

### It Directly Reduces Net Profit
Your strategy may be profitable on paper, but brokerage cuts into **realized returns**.

### High-Frequency Traders Are Hit the Hardest
Scalpers, option buyers, and intraday traders place **multiple orders daily** — brokerage compounds quickly.

### Small Trades Become Expensive
Buying multiple OTM option lots?  
Each order attracts a separate brokerage charge.

### It Influences Trading Behavior
Many traders avoid adjusting positions or hedging — not because it’s wrong, but because **brokerage makes it expensive**.

---

## The Industry Standard: Same Structure, Different Names

Let’s be honest — most brokers follow **almost identical brokerage models**.

### Traditional Brokerage Structure (Industry-Wide)

| Parameter | How It Works |
|--------|-------------|
| Charging Basis | Per order or per lot |
| Intraday | ₹20 or 0.03% per order |
| Options | Flat ₹20 per executed order |
| Futures | ₹20 or per-lot pricing |
| High Volume Benefit | ❌ No |
| Frequent Position Changes | ❌ Penalized |

This structure applies across major brokers like Zerodha, Fyers, and Dhan.

➡️ **Trade more = Pay more**, regardless of volume.

---

## The Problem With Per-Order & Per-Lot Pricing

### Punishes Active Traders
More trades don’t mean more risk — but they **do mean more brokerage**.

### Discourages Smart Adjustments
Rolling, hedging, or rebalancing becomes costly.

### Not Aligned With Real Trading Behavior
Modern traders:
- Scale in and out
- Trade multiple OTM strikes
- Adjust positions frequently

Yet the pricing model hasn’t evolved.

---
