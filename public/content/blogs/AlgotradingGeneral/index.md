## What Is Algo Trading?

Algo trading (short for algorithmic trading) means using a computer program to buy or sell stocks based on a set of rules.

For example:
> “Buy 100 shares of stock X if its price goes above ₹500 and volume is high.”

Instead of watching charts manually, a bot watches the market and places trades for you. It can do this in milliseconds, 24/7, without emotions or delay.

---

## Algo Trading in India: The Numbers

According to Angel One and NSE:

- **57% of equity cash trades** in India are now done by algos  
- **70% of derivatives trades** (F&O) are algo-based  
- A large part of this is driven by **retail traders**, not just institutions

In short, algo trading is now mainstream — and not just for big firms.

---

## Why Is Algo Trading Growing?

Several reasons:

- **Fast internet and APIs** let traders automate strategies easily  
- **Python and libraries** make it simple to code your own logic  
- **No-code platforms** let even non-coders use algorithms  
- **Broker support and SEBI rules** make algo trading safer and more accessible  
- **AI and ML tools** help find smarter signals and automate them  

Whether you're a coder or not, you now have ways to use algos.

---

## Two Ways to Start Algo Trading

You have two main options:

### 1. Build Your Own Algo (using Python)

You write your own code, connect to broker APIs, and run the bot yourself.

**Pros:**
- Full control and customization  
- Great for learning and experimenting  
- No monthly platform fees  
- Use any data, logic, or models you want  

**Cons:**
- Need to know Python (or learn it)  
- Takes time to build and test properly  
- You need to manage your server, errors, logs  
- Mistakes can be costly if not tested properly  

---

### 2. Use No-Code Platforms (like Tradetron, AlgoTest)

These are drag-and-drop tools where you define logic like:

> “Buy when RSI < 30 and volume > average.”

**Pros:**
- No coding needed  
- Fast to set up and deploy  
- Easy backtesting and live deployment  
- Works with multiple brokers  

**Cons:**
- Monthly or trade-based fees  
- Limited customization  
- Depends on platform uptime and support  
- May not support very complex logic  

---

## Which One Should You Use?

| Criteria         | Build Your Own (Python) | Use a Platform |
|------------------|-------------------------|----------------|
| Control          | ✅ Full                 | ❌ Limited     |
| Coding Needed    | ✅ Yes                 | ❌ No          |
| Cost             | ✅ Low recurring        | ❌ Monthly fee |
| Setup Time       | ❌ Slower               | ✅ Faster      |
| Custom Features  | ✅ Anything you want     | ❌ Platform-dependent |
| Support          | ❌ Self-managed          | ✅ Community/docs |

If you’re technical and like building, coding your own bot gives full freedom.  
If you want to go live quickly with lower effort, platforms make it easy.

---

## Final Thoughts

Algo trading has taken over most of India's markets — and retail traders are leading the charge.

Whether you code your own bot or use a platform:
- Start small  
- Backtest everything  
- Use UAT or paper trading first  
- Focus on risk, not just returns  

The tools are ready — it’s about choosing your path and building with care.
